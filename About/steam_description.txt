[h1]RimSynapse - Factions[/h1]
[b][url=https://github.com/RimSynapse/Factions/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]
[b]Procedurally generated faction histories, geopolitical schisms, and deep ruler profiles using LLMs.[/b]

[b]REQUIRES RimSynapse - Regions and Territories, and must load after it.[/b]

No more generic faction templates. Factions works alongside Regions and Territories to spawn unique, cloned sub-factions, each with their own procedurally generated histories, schisms, and cultural descriptions.

As of 0.7 this mod also owns the systems that decide what a faction is worth - regional economy (production and taxation against Empire Refactored's own figures), settlement size classification, military reach and supply, and a published faction standing surface other mods can read. Regions and Territories says what a world object is and where it may stand; Factions says what the faction holding it extracts, taxes and defends.

---

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Procedural Lore and Histories:[/b] Automatically generates rich, ideologically consistent lore and schisms for each spawned faction during world creation.
[*] [b]Geopolitical Context:[/b] Interacts with territory borders to spawn splinter houses and rival domains.
[*] [b]Faction Leader Profiling:[/b] Directly feeds Faction Leader details into the Psychology module for rich, interactive ruler personalities (backstories, traits, and agendas).
[*] [b]Thread-Safe Map Overlays:[/b] Displays visual, expandable territory control maps using the Map Mode Framework.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] RimSynapse - Core
[*] RimSynapse - Regions and Territories
[*] Harmony
[/list]

[b]Load order matters, and getting it wrong is silent.[/b] Core and Regions and Territories must both sit [b]above[/b] this mod in your list. Factions is compiled against Regions and Territories, so if it loads first it cannot resolve its own types - it will look active while doing nothing at all: no world generation step, no faction behaviour, no working settings.

RimWorld does not fix this for you. A mod's declared load order only advises the in-game mod list; the order in ModsConfig.xml is what actually loads, and any tool that sorts your list alphabetically produces the broken order, because "factions" sorts before "regions". RimSynapse Core now detects it at startup and writes a plain-language error naming both mods.
