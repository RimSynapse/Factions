[h1]RimSynapse - Factions[/h1]
[b]📚 [url=https://github.com/RimSynapse/Factions/wiki]Official Wiki &amp; Documentation[/url][/b]

[b]Companion Mod for RimSynapse.[/b]
Intercepts world generation to dynamically spawn cloned factions, establish geographic settlement territories, and generate deep LLM histories for the resulting empires and nations.

[h2]Changelog[/h2]
[b]v0.4.0[/b]
- Updated to support RimSynapse Core v0.4.0 (Multi-provider routing and Image generation).

[h2]Compatibility Matrix[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Royalty DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Enables royal splinter houses and vassalage generation.
[*] [b]Ideology DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Integrates holy wars and organically generated schisms.
[*] [b]Biotech DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Enables entire factions composed of xenotype warmongers.
[*] [b]Anomaly DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Generates hidden cult factions that worship the void.
[*] [b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3760830041]RimSynapse - Psychology[/url]:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Made better with Psychology! Automatically generates deep psychological profiles, traits, and backstories for Faction Leaders.
[*] [b]RimSynapse - WorldNews:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Generates dynamic newspapers detailing your factions' histories, holy wars, and geopolitical shifts.
[/list]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]RimSynapse - Core[/b] (Must be loaded before this mod)
[/list]
