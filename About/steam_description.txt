[h1]RimSynapse - Factions[/h1]
[b][url=https://github.com/RimSynapse/Factions/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]
[b]Procedurally generated faction histories, geopolitical schisms, and deep ruler profiles using LLMs.[/b]

No more generic faction templates. Factions works alongside Regions and Territories to spawn unique, cloned sub-factions, each with their own procedurally generated histories, schisms, and cultural descriptions.

---

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Procedural Lore and Histories:[/b] Automatically generates rich, ideologically consistent lore and schisms for each spawned faction during world creation.
[*] [b]Geopolitical Context:[/b] Interacts with territory borders to spawn splinter houses and rival domains.
[*] [b]Faction Leader Profiling:[/b] Directly feeds Faction Leader details into the Psychology module for rich, interactive ruler personalities (backstories, traits, and agendas).
[*] [b]Thread-Safe Map Overlays:[/b] Displays visual, expandable territory control maps using the Map Mode Framework.
[/list]

---

[h2]Roadmap[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]0.6 - Agent and Tool Foundation:[/b] An in-game agent that plans and carries out multi-step actions on its own, backed by a growing library of tools it can query for colony, faction and world state.
[*] [b]0.7 - Logic Externalization:[/b] Retiring hardcoded thresholds so the storyteller reasons about your colony's situation instead of following fixed rules.
[*] [b]0.8 - Emergent Systems and Content:[/b] Faction archetypes for each DLC, caravans that travel the world map, settlement crises, faction capitals and travelling leaders, and richer colonist social dynamics.
[/list]
These ship when they are ready - no dates.

[h2]Compatibility Matrix[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Royalty DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Enables royal splinter houses and vassalage generation.
[*] [b]Ideology DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Integrates holy wars and organically generated schisms.
[*] [b]Biotech DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Enables entire factions composed of xenotype warmongers.
[*] [b]Anomaly DLC:[/b] Compatible and Optional. Generates hidden cult factions that worship the void.
[*] [b]RimSynapse - Regions and Territories:[/b] Required. Generates the map provinces and borders.
[*] [b]RimSynapse - Psychology:[/b] Highly Recommended. Automatically generates deep psychological profiles, traits, and backstories for Faction Leaders.
[*] [b]RimSynapse - WorldNews:[/b] Highly Recommended. Generates dynamic newspapers detailing your factions' histories, holy wars, and geopolitical shifts.
[/list]
